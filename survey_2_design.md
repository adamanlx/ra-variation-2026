## English

Thank you for taking this survey! First, we'll make sure you're comfortable taking the survey and providing us with information.

**KEY INFORMATION**

* This project is studying ways that Kinyarwanda speakers say things differently.
* Your participation in this research project is completely voluntary.
* Your participation will take less than twenty minutes.
* You will be asked to read Kinyarwanda sentences and provide your judgements on them. More detailed information on the procedures can be found below.
* There are no possible risks or discomforts.
* You will receive 21,000 RWF over Momo.
* Your alternative to participating in this research study is not to participate.

**DETAILED INFORMATION**

**INTRODUCTION** The Department of Linguistics at the University of Kansas supports the practice of protection for human subjects participating in research. The following information is provided for you to decide whether you wish to participate in the present study. You may refuse to sign this form and not participate in this study. You should be aware that even if you agree to participate, you are free to withdraw at any time. If you do withdraw from this study, it will not affect your relationship with this unit, the services it may provide to you, or the University of Kansas.

**PURPOSE OF THE STUDY** This study is investigating the different ways that Kinyarwanda speakers say things.

**PROCEDURES** During the survey, you will be presented with a series of sentences. You will be asked whether you would say such a sentence, or whether you would be surprised if you heard such a sentence. You will be asked about your opinions on the Kinyarwanda language, whether people speak it differently, or whether the language is changing.

**RISKS** No risks are anticipated.

**PAYMENT TO PARTICIPANTS** You will receive 21,000 RWF upon completion of the study via Momo.

**PARTICIPANT CONFIDENTIALITY** Your name will not be associated in any publication or presentation with the information collected about you or with the research findings from this study. Your identifiable information will not be shared unless (a) it is required by law or university policy, or (b) you give written permission. Permission granted on this date to use and disclose your information remains in effect indefinitely. By signing this form you give permission for the use and disclosure of your information for purposes of this study at any time in the future.

**PRIVATE INFORMATION (DATA)** Your identifiable information may be removed from the data collected during this project, and the de-identified data will be used for future research without additional consent from you.

**REFUSAL TO SIGN CONSENT AND AUTHORIZATION** You are not required to sign this Consent and Authorization form and you may refuse to do so without affecting your right to any services you are receiving or may receive from the University of Kansas or to participate in any programs or events of the University of Kansas. However, if you refuse to sign, you cannot participate in this study.

**CANCELLING THIS CONSENT AND AUTHORIZATION** You may withdraw your consent to participate in this study at any time. You also have the right to cancel your permission to use and disclose further information collected about you, in writing, at any time, by sending your written request to Adam An, at aan@ku.edu. If you cancel permission to use your information, the researchers will stop collecting additional information about you. However, the research team may use and disclose information that was gathered before they received your cancellation, as described above.

**QUESTIONS ABOUT PARTICIPATION** Questions about procedures should be directed to Adam An, at aan@ku.edu.

**PARTICIPATION CERTIFICATION** I have read this Consent and Authorization form. I have had the opportunity to ask, and I have received answers to, any questions I had regarding the study. I understand that if I have any additional questions about my rights as a research participant, I may call (785) 864-7429 or (785) 864-7385, write the Human Research Protection Program (HRPP), University of Kansas, 2385 Irving Hill Road, Lawrence, Kansas 66045-7568, or email irb@ku.edu.

# Instructions

Mu kinyarwanda inshinga ikoreshejwe mu nteruro akenshi iba ifitemo -ra-.Urugero, uhereye ku ijambo' kugwa' ushobora kuvuga uti 'imvura iragwa'. Icyo ubu bushakashatsi bugaminye ni ukumenya igihe abantu bakoresha -ra-. (In Kinyarwanda, often the verb in a sentence will have a -ra-. For example, from the word "kugwa", you might say "Imvura iragwa." The purpose of this study is to investigate when people use -ra-.)

Turifuza kumenya uko abantu bakoresha -ra- mu kinyarwanda cyabo cya buri munsi. Akenshi uburyo abantu bakoresha ururimi binyuranye n'ibyo uvuga cyangwa ibyigishwa mu ishuri. Icyo twifuza si uburyo bwiza byo gukoresha -ra-. Ahubwo ni ukemenya uko wowe ubwawe ukoresha -ra-,  n'uko wumva abandi bari bakwiye kuyikoresha. (We are interested in how people are using -ra- in day-to-day Kinyarwanda. Often, the way other people use language naturally differs from what you say or what is taught in school. We are not interested in what the “correct” way to use ra- is. Rather, we are interested in how you personally use ra-, and how you would expect others to use them.)

Muri ubu bushakashatsi, turaguha igihe n'interuro. Icyo usabwa ni ukureba inshinga ukavuga niba wakoresha iyo nteruro mu gihe uvuga ikintu kirimo kuba mu gihe wahawe. (In this study, we will give you a time and a sentence. Your task is to look at the verb and decide whether you would use the sentence to talk about something happening at the given time.)

Urugero: iyo ubonye: (For example: if you see:)

**IGIHE: icyumweru gitaha** (TIME: next week)

**Nzajya** mu Rwanda. (**I will go** to Rwanda.)

Ibi wabivuga kubera ko 'nzajya' bishobora kuzaba mu cyumweru gitaha. (You probably say this, because 'nzajya' can happen next week.)

Ariko iyo ubonye: (However, if you see:)

**IGIHE: icyumweru gitaha** (TIME: next week)

**Natetse** ibitoki. (**I ate** plantains.)

Birashoboka ko ibi utabivuga kubera ko 'natetse' bidashobora kuba mu cyumweru gitaha. (You probably don't say this, because 'natetse' cannot happen next week.)

N'igihe ubonye: (And if you see:)

**IGIHE: icyumweru gitaha**

**Nzarajya** mu Rwanda. (**I will go** to Rwanda.)

Ushobora kuba ibi utabivuga kubera ko mu kinyarwanda udashobora kuvuga ngo "nzarajya". (You probably don't say this, because you cannot say 'nzarajya' in Kinyarwanda.)

Turatangira dukora umwitozo wo kwimenyereza inshuro eshatu kugira ngo twizere ko wumvise icyo usabwa gukora. (We will start with three practice rounds to make sure you understand the task.)

# Practice rounds

The first practice round was presented on the same page as the instructions above. The remaining two practice rounds were presented on individual pages. On the last page, we presented the following text:

Noneho dutangire dukore akazi dusabwa. Izere neza ko usobanukiwe n'akazi usabwe gukora mbere yo gukomeza. Ku nteruro zimwe biragorana gufata umwanzuro. Wibuke ko nta gisubizo kiri cyo giteganijwe. Icyo twifuza ni ukumenya uko wowe ubwawe ukoresha -ra-, n'uko wumva abantu bari bakwiye kuyikoresha. (Now we will begin the task. Please make sure you understand the task before continuing. Some of these sentences will be more difficult to decide. Remember, there are no correct answers. We are interested in how you personally use -ra-, and how you would expect others to use them.)

Practice stimuli were presented using the same format as experimental stimuli as detailed below, excepting the "do you have any other thoughts about this sentence?" free-response question.

# Experimental stimuli

Twibanze ku nshinga, wowe ubwawe ushobora kuvuga interuro nk'iyi mu gihe urimo kuvuga ikintu kiba mugihe wahawe? (Focusing on the verb, would you yourself say the sentence like this when describing something happening at the given time?) (multiple-choice question)

1. Buri gihe ni ko nayivuga. (I would always say the sentence like that.)
2. Akenshi ni ko nayivuga. (I would often say the sentence like that.)
3. Simbizi. (I don't know.)
4. Hari ubwo nayivuga gutyo. (I would occasionally say the sentence like that.)
5. Nta na rimwe nayivuga gutyo. (I would never say the sentence like that.)

Mu gihe tugitekereza ku nshinga: byagutangaza ku ruhe rugero uramutse wunvise undi muntu uvuga ikinyarwanda avuze interuro nk'iyi mu gihe avuga ku kintu mu gihe runaka? (Still thinking about the verb: How surprised would you be if you heard another Kinyarwanda speaker say this sentence like this when describing something happening at the given time?) (multiple-choice question)

1. Byantangaza cyane. Nta muntu n'umwe ndumva abivuga atyo. (I would be extremely surprised. I have never heard someone say it like that.)
2. Byantangaza biringaniye. Ndumva bicocamye. (I would be quite surprised. It sounds weird to me.)
3. Sinzi neza niba byantangaza cyangwa bitantangaza. (I am unsure whether it would surprise me or not.)
4. Ntabwo byantangaza pe. Nkunze kumva abantu babivuga gutyo. (I wouldn't really be surprised. I have heard people say things in the same way.)
5. Ntabwo byantangaza na gato. Kuri njye ndumva ari ibisanzwe. (I would not be at all surprised. It sounds normal to me.)

Hari ikindi waba utekereza kuri iyi nteruro? (Do you have any other thoughts about this sentence?) (free-response question)
