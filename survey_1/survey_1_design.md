## Kinyarwanda

**AMAKURU ARAMBUYE**

**INTANGIRIRO**
Agashami k'iyigandimi ka Kaminuza ya Kansas gashyigikira igikorwa cyo kurinda abantu bagira uruhare mu bushakashatsi. Amakuru akurikira agamije kugufasha kumenya niba wafata umwanzuro wo kugira uruhare muri ubu bushakashatsi. Ushobora guhitamo kwanga gusinya iyi fishi maze ntugire uruhare muri ubu bushakashatsi. Menya ko n'igihe wemeye kugira uruhare muri ubu bushakashatsi, ufite uburenganzira bwo kubireka igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Nubireka, ntacyo bizahindura ku mibanire yawe n'abakora ubushakashatsi, kuri serivisi baguha cyangwa se ku mibanire yawe na Kaminuza ya Kansas.

**ICYO UBU BUSHAKASHATSI BUGAMIJE**
Ubu bushakashatsi bugamije kumenya uburyo abavuga ikinyarwanda bavuga ibintu ku buryo butandukanye.

**UKO BIGENDA**
Mu gihe cy'ubushakashatsi, urahabwa urutonde rw'interuro. Urabazwa niba wavuga interuro runaka, cyangwa niba byagutangaza wumvise umuntu avuze iyo nteruro. Urabazwa ibyo utekereza ku rurimi rw'ikinyarwanda, niba abantu bakivuga ku buryo butandukanye, cyangwa niba uru rurimi rurimo kugenda ruhinduka.

**INKURIKIZI**
Nta nkurikizi zitezwe.

**KWISHYURA UZAGIRA URUHARE MURI UBU BUSHAKASHATSI**
Uzahabwa amafaranga 7000 kuri momo ubu bushakashatsi burangiye.

**KUGIRIRA IBANGA UWITABIRIYE UBU BUSHAKASHATSI**
Izina ryawe ntaho rizagaragara mu bizatangazwa cyangwa bizamurikwa hashingiwe ku makuru yaguturutseho cyangwa ibizaba byavuye muri ubu bushakashatsi. Amakuru akwerekeyeho ntawe azahabwa kiretse (a) biri mu bisabwa na politiki ya kaminuza cyangwa (b) ubitangiye uburenganzira mu nyandiko.

Uburenganzira utanze kuri iyi tariki bwo gukoresha no gutangaza amakuru yawe bugumaho igihe cyose. Gusinya iyi fishi bivuze ko utanze uburenganzira bwo gukoresha no gutangaza amakuru yawe mu rwego rw'ubu bushakashatsi igihe icyo ari cyo cyose mu gihe kizaza.

**AMAKURU AKWEREKEYEHO**
Amakuru akwerekeyeho yatuma umwirondoro wawe umenyekana ashobora gukurwa mu makuru yakusanyijwe muri uyu mushinga, noneho amakuru atagaragaza umwirondoro akazakoreshwa mu bushakashatsi mu gihe kizaza, hatabayeho kongera kugusaba kwemera ko atangazwa.

**KWANGA GUSINYA IYI FISHI YO KWEMERA KUGIRA URUHARE MU BUSHAKASHATSI NO GUTANGA UBURENGANZIRA**
Ntabwo utegetswe gusinya iyi fishi yo kwemera kugira uruhare mu bushakashatsi no gutanga uburenganzira, ushobora no kwanga kubikora ntibigire icyo bihindura ku burengenzira ufite bwo guhabwa serivisi za Kaminuza ya Kansas cyangwa bwo kugira uruhare muri gahunda cyangwa ibikorwa bya Kaminuza ya Kansas. Gusa, iyo wanze gusinya, ntushobora kugira uruhare muri ubu bushakashatsi.

**GUHAGARIKA KWEMERA KUGIRA URUHARE MU BUSHAKASHATSI NO GUTANGA UBURENGANZIRA**
Ushobora guhagarika kwemera kugira uruhare muri ubu bushakashatsi igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Ufite kandi uburengenzira bwo guhagarika uburengenzira watanze bwo gukomeza gutangaza amakuru akwerekeyeho, ukabikora mu nyandiko, igihe icyo ari cyo cyose, woherereza ubusabe bwawe Adam An kuri aan@ku.edu.

Nuhagarika uburenganzira bwo gukoresha amakuru yawe, abashakashatsi bazahagarika igikorwa cyo gukusanya andi makuru akwerekeyeho. Gusa, itsinda rikora ubushakashatsi rishobora gukoresha kandi rigatangaza amakuru yegeranijwe mbere y'uko babona ubusabe bwawe bwo guhagarika, nk'uko byasobanuwe haruguru.

## English

**KEY INFORMATION**

* This project is studying ways that Kinyarwanda speakers say things differently.
* Your participation in this research project is completely voluntary.
* Your participation will take less than twenty minutes.
* You will be asked to read Kinyarwanda sentences and provide your judgements on them. More detailed information on the procedures can be found below.
* There are no possible risks or discomforts.
* You will receive 7000 RWF over Momo.
* Your alternative to participating in this research study is not to participate.

**DETAILED INFORMATION**

**INTRODUCTION**
The Department of Linguistics at the University of Kansas supports the practice of protection for human subjects participating in research. The following information is provided for you to decide whether you wish to participate in the present study. You may refuse to sign this form and not participate in this study. You should be aware that even if you agree to participate, you are free to withdraw at any time. If you do withdraw from this study, it will not affect your relationship with this unit, the services it may provide to you, or the University of Kansas.

**PURPOSE OF THE STUDY**
This study is investigating the different ways that Kinyarwanda speakers say things.

**PROCEDURES**
During the survey, you will be presented with a series of sentences. You will be asked whether you would say such a sentence, or whether you would be surprised if you heard such a sentence. You will be asked about your opinions on the Kinyarwanda language, whether people speak it differently, or whether the language is changing.

**RISKS**
No risks are anticipated.

**PAYMENT TO PARTICIPANTS**
You will receive 7000 RWF upon completion of the study over Momo.

**PARTICIPANT CONFIDENTIALITY**
Your name will not be associated in any publication or presentation with the information collected about you or with the research findings from this study. Your identifiable information will not be shared unless (a) it is required by law or university policy, or (b) you give written permission.

Permission granted on this date to use and disclose your information remains in effect indefinitely. By signing this form you give permission for the use and disclosure of your information for purposes of this study at any time in the future.

**PRIVATE INFORMATION (DATA)**
Your identifiable information may be removed from the data collected during this project, and the de-identified data will be used for future research without additional consent from you.

**REFUSAL TO SIGN CONSENT AND AUTHORIZATION**
You are not required to sign this Consent and Authorization form and you may refuse to do so without affecting your right to any services you are receiving or may receive from the University of Kansas or to participate in any programs or events of the University of Kansas. However, if you refuse to sign, you cannot participate in this study.

**CANCELLING THIS CONSENT AND AUTHORIZATION**
You may withdraw your consent to participate in this study at any time. You also have the right to cancel your permission to use and disclose further information collected about you, in writing, at any time, by sending your written request to Adam An, at aan@ku.edu.

If you cancel permission to use your information, the researchers will stop collecting additional information about you. However, the research team may use and disclose information that was gathered before they received your cancellation, as described above.

**QUESTIONS ABOUT PARTICIPATION**
Questions about procedures should be directed to Adam An, at aan@ku.edu.

# Kinyarwanda

Muri iki gice, dukeneye kumenya icyo wowe ubwawe utekereza ku kinyarwanda. Geregeza gusubiza ibibazo byinshi bishoboka. Nta gisubizo kiri cyo giteganijwe, bityo rero usubize wisanzuye kandi ukoreshe ukuri kwawe. Niba ufite impungenge zo gusubiza ibibazo bimwe, wabisimbuka. Ushobora gusubiza mu kinyarwanda cyangwa mu cyongereza. (In these sections, we are interested in your personal thoughts and opinions about Kinyarwanda.  Please answer as many questions as you can. There are no correct answers, so please feel free to answer openly and honestly. If you are uncomfortable answering any questions, you may leave them blank. You may answer in Kinyarwanda or English.)

Waba warabonye ko abandi bantu bavuga ikinyarwanda ku buryo butandukanye? Niba ari byo, bimwe mu bintu abantu bavuga ku buryo butandukanye ni ibihe? (Have you ever noticed that other people speak Kinyarwanda differently? If so, what are some things that people say differently?)

Ni uwuhe muntu ukeka ko avuga ikinyarwanda kiboneye? Ukeka ko uvuga ikinyarwanda kiboneye? (What kind of person do you think speaks standard Kinyarwanda? Do you think you speak standard Kinyarwanda?)

Ni abahe bantu ukeka ko bavuga ikinyarwanda kitaboneye? Ushobora kutubwira ikintu bavuga utekereza ko atari ikinyarwanda kiboneye? (What kind of person do you think speaks nonstandard Kinyarwanda? Can you think of anything they say that is nonstandard?)

Ni ryari abantu bakoresha ikinyarwanda kiboneye cyangwa ikinyarwanda kitaboneye? Hari igihe ukoresha ikinyarwanda kiboneye cyangwa igihe ukoresha ikinyarwanda kitaboneye? (What situations or times do people use standard Kinyarwanda or nonstandard Kinyarwanda? Do you have times when you use standard Kinyarwanda or nonstandard Kinyarwanda?)

Ukeka ko ikinyarwanda kirimo guhinduka? (Do you think Kinyarwanda is changing?)

Abantu bakomoka mu bice bitandukanye by'u Rwanda bavuga ku buryo butandukanye? Ushobora gutekereza ikintu abantu bavuga mu gice kimwe gusa cy'u Rwanda? (Do people from different parts of Rwanda speak differently? Can you think of anything that people only say in one part of Rwanda?)

Urubyiruko n'abantu bakuru bavuga mu buryo butandukanye? Urugero: Hari ibintu uvuga ku buryo butandukanye n'uburyo ababyeyi bawe bavuga? Ufite abana ukaba ubona ko muvuga ku buryo butandukanye? (Do young and old people speak differently? For example, do you say anything differently from your parents? Do you have children and notice that they speak differently?)

Abagore n'abagabo baba bavuga ku buryo butandukanye? Hari ibintu bivugwa n'abagore n'ibivugwa n'abagabo? (Do men and women speak differently? Are there things that men say or that women say?)

# Sentences

Ushobora gusubiza mu kinyarwanda cyangwa mu cyongereza. (You may answer in Kinyarwanda or English.)

Interuro ni izi: (Here is a sentence:)

(sentence a)

Abantu bamwe interuro imwe bashobora kuyivuga ku buryo bukurikira: (Some people might say the same sentence like this:)

(sentence b)

Sentences were as follows:

|variable of interest|sentence a|sentence b|
|-|-|-|
|CJ verbs before *ngo*|Ejo, waravuze ngo, "hanze hijimye."|Ejo, wavuze ngo, "hanze hijimye."|
|progressive *ra-* in negation|Aka kanya, imvura ntiri kugwa.|Aka kanya, imvura ntiragwa.|
|periphrastic|Aka kanya, ndatekereza.|Aka kanya, ndi gutekereza.|
|*g-*|Umwembe umwe waguye hasi.|Umwembe gumwe gwaguye hasi.|

Wowe ubwawe, interuro ya kabiri wayivuga? (Would you yourself say the second sentence?)

* Buri gihe ni ko nayivuga. (I would always say the sentence like that.)
* Akenshi ni ko nayivuga. (I would often say the sentence like that.)
* Simbizi. (I don't know.)
* Hari ubwo nayivuga gutyo. (I would occasionally say the sentence like that.)
* Nta na rimwe nayivuga gutyo. (I would never say the sentence like that.)

Hari umuntu wigeze kumva avuga interuro ya kabiri? (Have you heard anyone else say the second sentence?)

Niba ari byo, abantu babivuga gutyo ni bantu ki? Mu gihe usoma iyi nteruro, hari umuntu waba utekereje uvuga gutyo? (If so, what kind of person says it this way? Do you think of anyone when you read this sentence?)

Ese hari igihe runaka wumva ukwiye kuvuga imwe muri izi nteruro? (Is there any time when you would prefer to say one sentence or the other? Is anything wrong with either sentence?)

Waba utekereza aho izi nteruro zitandukaniye? (Do you have any other thoughts on the difference between these sentences?)

Hari icyo waba utekereza ku gihe  abantu bavuga amagambo arimo "ra" nko kuvuga ngo "iragwa", 'ntiragwa"? Hari icyo utekereza ku gihe batayishyiramo? (Do you have any other thoughts about when people say words that have "ra" inside them like "iragwa" or "ntiragwa"? Do you have any thoughts about when people leave it out?)

Hari icyo waba utekereza ku byerekeye igihe abantu bakoresha amagambo nka 'gumwe' cyangwa 'gwaguye', atangirwa na g? (Do you have any other thoughts about when people use words like "gumwe" or "gwaguye" that start with g?)
